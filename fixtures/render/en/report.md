## Executive summary

**Bottom line:** some AI assistants stop at the door of the bakery's site, and those that get in do not find a clear picture of the business: who it is, where the branches are and when they are open.

**The good news:** the content itself is good. Product pages carry a description, weight and ingredients.

**The proposal:** a short working session to choose 2–3 focused improvements together, without changing the site's design.

---

## 1. Why this matters

When a customer asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity "which bakery near me is open now", the assistant answers from the sources it can read and understand. In Hebrew: מאפייה פתוחה עכשיו.

## 2. What we found

| # | Finding | Business impact |
|---|---------|-----------------|
| 1 | **Access is inconsistent.** Some cloud locations get HTTP 403 before reading a word. | Nobody can tell in advance which assistant will see the bakery. |
| 2 | **No structured business identity.** Only generic WebPage and WebSite data was found. | An assistant has to guess the opening hours from the text. |
| 3 | **No llms.txt.** There is no short guide for AI agents. | A cheap, simple way to point assistants at the key pages. |

## 3. What works today

- Rich product pages: description, weight and ingredients.
- Branch pages with an address and opening hours.

## 4. Next step

A short working session with whoever manages the site.
